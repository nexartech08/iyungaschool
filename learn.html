IYUNGA SECONDARY SCHOOL

Welcome to Iyunga secondary school.

Iyunga Technical Secondary School, located in Mbeya, Tanzania, is a specialized institution offering technical and vocational education alongside the standard secondary curriculum.
The school aims to equip students with practical skills and knowledge, preparing them for careers in technical fields and higher education.
The school offers a variety of advanced-level subject combinations, including PCM(Physics, Chemistry, Advanced Mathematics), PCB(Physics, Chemistry, Biology), PcoM(Physics, Computer, Advanced Mathematics), and many others.

Advanced-level Subject Combinations

The complete list of offered subject combinations:
  1. Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics (PCM).
  2. Physics, Chemistry and Biology (PCB).
  3. Physsics, Geography and Mathematics (PGM).
